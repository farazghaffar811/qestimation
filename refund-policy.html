Refund Policy

We always strive to provide our clients maximum satisfaction and value-for-money services. However, please note that Qestimation does not issue refunds. We offer exchanges only – if a deliverable does not meet the agreed scope we will revise or re-issue it until it does.

Payment security. We do not take credit card or payment-related details. None of our staff are allowed or authorised to collect card or payment details from clients. We send an invoice and clients process the payment themselves through the options below.
