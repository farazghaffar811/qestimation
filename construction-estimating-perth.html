Qestimation in Perth

Perth's residential market is running at record lead times while resources projects pull skilled labour north. Estimating in Western Australia means pricing scarcity as carefully as quantities.

WA builders face the longest material lead times in the country and labour costs inflated by the mining sector. Our Perth takeoffs flag long-lead items early and price against current Western Australian supplier quotes so your tender survives the gap between pricing and procurement.

What we estimate in Perth

Recent Perth work includes single and double-storey housing across the northern and southern corridors, apartment projects in the CBD and Subiaco, industrial facilities in Kwinana and Henderson, school and health upgrades, and regional mining-town accommodation. Whether you are a head contractor pricing a tender, a subcontractor quoting a trade package or a developer testing feasibility, you receive the same deliverables: a trade-by-trade quantity takeoff, colour-coded marked plans, an executive summary and a priced estimate in Excel.

Perth estimating FAQ

How much does a construction estimate cost in Perth?

Single-trade takeoffs for Perth projects start in the low hundreds of dollars; full multi-trade priced estimates are quoted as a fixed fee after we review your drawings. Request a free quote and you will have a price within 24 hours.

Do you price Perth projects with local rates?

Yes. Every Perth estimate uses current Perth and Western Australia supplier quotes and subcontractor rates, not national averages, so your tender reflects what the work actually costs in WA.

How quickly can you turn around a Perth takeoff?

Most trade takeoffs are delivered in 2 to 5 business days. Rush service is available for tenders closing sooner.

Do you need to visit the site in Perth?

No. Estimates are prepared from your drawings, specifications and reports, so we serve all of Perth CBD, Joondalup, Rockingham, Mandurah, the eastern suburbs and regional WA mining towns remotely with the same turnaround.
