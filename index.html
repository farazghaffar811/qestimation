Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about construction estimating, turnaround and how we work.

What is a construction cost estimate?

A construction cost estimate is a detailed forecast of what a building project will cost, covering materials, labour, equipment, overheads and contingencies. Qestimation prepares conceptual, bid and budget estimates so developers and contractors know the real number before committing.

How accurate are Qestimation estimates?

Our estimates are typically within 1% of final construction costs. We price every item from current supplier and subcontractor rates for the project’s location and cross-check quantities with colour-coded marked plans.

How fast can I get a quote or estimate?

A free quote is usually returned within 24 hours of receiving your drawings. Most trade takeoffs are delivered in 2–5 business days depending on project size, and rush turnaround is available.

Which file formats can I send?

We accept PDF, DWG, DXF, Revit/BIM exports, scanned plans and photos. Upload them through the quote form or email them to our estimating team.

Who uses Qestimation?

Developers, general contractors, subcontractors, architects, quantity surveyors and owner-builders across Australia use Qestimation for quantity takeoffs, bid estimates, budget estimates and on-site cost control.

What trades do you estimate?

Site work, concrete, masonry, structural and miscellaneous metals, lumber and framing, drywall, flooring, painting, exterior finishes, plumbing, electrical, HVAC and more. See our work samples for real examples.

Do you offer refunds?

We do not issue refunds; instead we offer exchanges and revisions until the deliverable meets the agreed scope. Full details are on our Refund Policy page.
